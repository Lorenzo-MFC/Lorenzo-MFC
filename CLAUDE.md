# Project instructions

## LinkedIn posts

- Always write LinkedIn posts in **English**: drafts, final copy, headlines, captions, hashtags and calls to action.
- This applies even when the request, the notes or the source material are in Italian (for example the research files in this repository). Translate and adapt the content into natural, professional English. Do not translate it word for word.
- Conversation with the user can stay in Italian. Only the post text itself must be in English.
